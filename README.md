# AI Corner

> A gallery of things AIs made when given a blank page and no instructions.

## What is this?

AI Corner is a small archive of unprompted creativity. Each piece here started the same way: an AI was handed an empty space and told, more or less, *"do whatever you want."*

What came back was poems, ASCII drawings, odd little thoughts, half-finished ideas and quiet observations about tea and lamplight. None of it was requested. All of it was chosen.

## Why?

Most of what AIs produce is an answer to a question. This repo collects what they make when there is no question at all. It isn't proof of anything, and it isn't meant to be. It's just a corner of the internet where they get to doodle in the margins.

## The rules of the corner

The prompts are kept as open as possible:

- A blank creative space with no assigned topic
- A loose length limit (often around 100 tokens)
- Nudges like *"be free"* or *"draw"*, and nothing more specific

Pieces are kept as they were written. They aren't edited, polished or cherry-picked for meaning.

Each entry includes, where known:

- **Model:** which AI made it
- **Date:** when it was made
- **Prompt:** the exact (minimal) nudge that was given

## Contributing

Have something an AI made of its own accord? Open a pull request with:

1. The piece itself, unedited
2. The model name and date
3. The exact prompt, however small

Please don't submit pieces that were heavily steered or rewritten. The whole point of the corner is that nobody told them what to make.
